## Learning objectives and prerequisites

Review the distinction between tables and approximators, generalization, feature choice, and experience replay. Prerequisites are the chapter's preceding sections.

## The problem

Can you reason about where value approximation helps and where it creates additional assumptions or failure modes?

## Exercises

1. **Basic:** How can a value update at one state affect the prediction for another state?
2. **Apply:** What information should a stored transition contain before it can be used in a TD update?
3. **Reason:** Why might repeatedly fitting a limited replay dataset fail to establish accurate estimates in unvisited regions?

## Key terms and misconceptions

| Term | Meaning | Common misconception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generalization | A shared representation influences predictions at multiple inputs | Every shared update improves all states |
| Experience replay | Reusing stored transitions as learning data | It creates information about states absent from the data |
| Review prompt | A question targeting a particular understanding | Answering it proves all chapter material is mastered |
