# 7.6 Summary: connect evaluation, improvement, and experience

[Chapter 7 PDF, pages 24–25](../../../3%20-%20Chapter%207%20Temporal-Difference%20Methods.pdf#page=24), printed pages 148–149. Review the target table in [Section 7.5](07-05.md) first.

TD learning updates an estimate using a reward and a continuation estimate. Under a fixed policy, the chapter's TD evaluation and Sarsa targets follow that policy. Adding policy improvement turns evaluation into a control procedure. Q-learning uses a maximizing continuation to target the Bellman optimality equation, while its behavior may explore differently.

An $n$-step target lets more rewards arrive before bootstrapping. In an episode ending early, it uses the remaining rewards and stops at the terminal state. Experience must reach the state–action pairs a task needs: a greedy target alone cannot supply unobserved transitions. The source also points ahead to importance sampling and TD($\lambda$), which extend these ideas.
